What to watch, by test scenario

1
Forward pushRegister a patient at a site. Watch the dot travel to Central and that site's Events received climb.
2
Referral / on-demand pullHydrate a patient into the other site (not shown here). Then any further edit at either site pushes normally — watch the dot again.
3
Offline / reconnectDisconnect a site's agent from the wan network. Watch its Unsent climb and Oldest unsent age. Reconnect — watch it drain back to zero.
4
Conflict handlingEdit the same field at both sites quickly. Watch Conflicts open or auto-resolved move.
5
ReconciliationWait for the scheduled interval. Watch each site's Last reconciliation move off "—".
6
Duplicate detectionRegister two patients with the same name, sex and birthdate at different sites. Watch Possible duplicates increase.
7
Central correctionPOST to /admin/corrections. Watch a purple ring pulse on Central itself (it originates there) and a row appear in Central corrections below.
8
Raw edit at central (what not to do)Run a plain SQL UPDATE directly against central-db. Nothing on this screen moves — no pulse, no dot, no row anywhere. That silence is the point: it proves the edit is invisible to sync.
